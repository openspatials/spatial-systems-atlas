Territory atlas

Covered counts a capability as covered when at least one of the subjects being counted reaches it: built in for the first number, built in or through an extension for the second, and those two or partial for the third. Production subjects counts only the subjects switched on whose status is shipping, ratified, board-approved or mixed; All subjects on counts every subject switched on. Must-interoperate rows only keeps those production subjects and narrows the rows to the ones two independent systems have to agree on. It says somebody has built the thing at least once, not that two systems work together, so read it as a floor, not a verdict.
